Local implementation and verification, October 9, 2026. No publication, upload or new artwork generation occurred. This pass continues the 1.5 controls/save, 1.6 distinct-method and 1.7 research-evidence work.

## What changed

1. Amazon and Florida angling setup now shows relative casting distance, the selected habitat/kit/lure, a marked accurate band and placement feedback. Untimed players can choose a distance with a native slider or deliberately use the guide placement. The confirmed choice uses the existing cast accuracy and encounter routing. The diagram is a one-dimensional schematic; it does not claim depth selection or two-dimensional aiming.
2. New peacock bass encounters alternate open-water bursts, turns from cover, measured recovery and surface rolls. Snook add a current-assisted surge and another cover turn. Seatrout have shorter bursts and headshake interruptions. Explicit season IDs adjust selected window lengths. These are authored game behaviors, not measured fish physiology or real handling instruction.
3. Hold to guide away from cover or recover line; release through bursts, rolls and headshakes. Guiding gives modest progress, recovery gives more, and missed responses can return distance, add mistakes or break the line. Each window offers half a second of reaction grace. Continuous holding cannot finish; doing nothing cannot finish. The crew abstracts rod-angle/drag adjustments into one accessible control.
4. The simulation, countdown, next cue, instruction, untimed action and fish pose derive from one saved behavior clock. Untimed mode advances exactly one window, including adjacent windows with the same action. Fish shape, movement, line load and habitat overlays are distinct but explicitly schematic. Pause and reduced-motion preferences disable decorative animation/transition. The prominent timed button changes between HOLD TO GUIDE, HOLD TO RECOVER and RELEASE THE CONTROL. Distinct missed-response feedback stays visible.
5. Schema 12 records the per-cast rules and beat. Genuine version 11 reviewed and mid-fight fixtures retain original fields, observations, first answers and evidence reviews. Older active encounters remain classic, including retries; eligible fresh casts get the new pilot. Storage preserves exact migration originals and recognized older recovery pointers. Draft, unconfirmed distance remains local component state; confirmed cast power is portable.
6. Browser testing fixed low-contrast diagram labels and a short-landscape reading pane that was only 22 pixels high. Pilot screens at 600–760 pixels wide and at most 550 pixels high now place the reading console beside the scene. Other interaction layouts are unchanged.
