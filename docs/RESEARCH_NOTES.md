# Research and Review Boundary

Living Waters is an educational simulation, not biological, conservation, access, or fishing advice. The playable text distinguishes ecological explanation from current local rules and links players to an authority from every discovered journal subject.

## Public source ledger

`src/data/sourceLedger.ts` records the release source for all fifteen destinations. Each entry contains:

- the issuing authority;
- a direct HTTPS link;
- the claim or design boundary it supports;
- an access date; and
- a review status checked by the content validator.

The ledger prioritizes national or regional agencies, intergovernmental bodies, protected-area authorities, and scientific institutions. Supporting source notes remain in each venue record so important uncertainties are visible beside the playable material rather than hidden in a separate bibliography.

## Scope of review

The public release checks that:

1. Featured organisms and habitats are plausible for the named region.
2. Seasonal mechanics reflect a documented environmental driver.
3. Protected wildlife is represented as observation or conservative release, not as a trophy.
4. Handling choices teach broad harm-reduction principles without claiming to replace local procedure.
5. Current permits, closures, protected areas, access rights, and gear rules remain explicitly outside the simulation.

## Known limits

- Guide and community characters are fictional narrative composites.
- Encounter probabilities are designed for varied play and are not population estimates.
- Seasonal states simplify systems that vary by year, reach, weather, and management.
- Generated catch reconstructions illustrate encounters; they are not evidence for scientific claims.
- Lake Holiday is private community water. The game intentionally directs players to current local notices instead of asserting access or retention rules.

## Contributor gate

A new venue is not release-ready until it has complete source-ledger metadata, internally consistent encounter rules, media-rights status, and passing validation tests. Material with uncertain occurrence, protection, handling, or provenance remains out of the public build.
