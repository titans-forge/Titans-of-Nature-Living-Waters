# Living Waters 1.15.0 — Underwater expeditions

Fish fights now move beneath the surface in fourteen fishing waters. Species-aware movement, submerged habitat artwork, pressure pulses and rod-side decisions make the encounter more readable and more skillful. Camera surveys, telemetry and other non-angling fieldwork retain their own mechanics.

The release adds thirteen habitat plates and fourteen fish atlases with 123 new views for 41 additional fish encounter IDs. These are educational reconstructions with enhanced visibility, not documentary images or measured fish physiology.

Clearer reel/release instructions, short-pulse coaching, pause/reload recovery and compact controls accompany save schema13. Older journeys migrate with recovery backups; active classic/v1 encounters retain their rules. Untimed assistance is still available and is recorded.

Validation: 410 tests across 32 suites, TypeScript, production build and media provenance passed. Native play covered all fourteen fishing waters during the rollout, with additional fish-family, coaching, save-import and packaged-build checks in the final pass. Astra at Max independently reviewed source and engine behavior. The full automated browser suite, physical-device checks and independent new-player testing remain outstanding.

Playing remains free. The existing Forge Game Hosting License, prior grants and third-party/media terms are preserved. ZIP includes the full required license/dependency notices.
